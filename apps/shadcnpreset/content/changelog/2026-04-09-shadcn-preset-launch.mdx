shadcnpreset launches with a simple goal: helping you find and explore shadcn presets in seconds, not hours of trial-and-error.

There are ~1.5 billion possible preset combinations, but no easy way to browse, compare, or see what people actually like.

This release introduces a community-driven preset library for shadcn — free and open source.

Features include:

- Smart search across presets by keyword, style, and vibe
- Save and manage your own presets
- Community voting to surface the most useful combinations
